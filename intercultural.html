Intercultural Engagement

Intercultural engagement means learning from people who have different cultures, backgrounds, and life experiences. To me, it is important because it helps people understand each other better and communicate with more respect.

As an international student, I have been able to meet many people from different places. I have participated in many EESA programs, and those experiences helped me learn more about different cultures, traditions, and ways of thinking. I was not only learning about others, but also sharing parts of my own background.

These programs also helped me become more confident talking with people I did not know before. I learned that simple things like listening, asking questions, and being respectful can make a big difference when people come from different backgrounds.

Overall, intercultural engagement has helped me grow as a person. It has made me more open-minded and more comfortable working with people from different cultures. I know these skills will be important in my future career because technology brings people from many backgrounds together.
