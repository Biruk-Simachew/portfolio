Research, Scholarly, & Creative Activity

As an ICT major, I have been able to work on different class projects that helped me understand how technology is used in real situations. These projects gave me a chance to practice problem-solving, research, and creative thinking while learning more about the technology field.

Some of my projects focused on data analysis, where I worked with information, looked for patterns, and used the results to make better decisions. These assignments helped me become more comfortable with organizing data, thinking carefully about problems, and explaining my findings in a clear way.

I have also worked on creative technology projects, including a mobile game project. Building a game helped me see how planning, design, coding, and testing all work together. It also taught me that even a small project can take patience, teamwork, and attention to detail.

Overall, these class projects have helped me grow as a student and as a future technology professional. They have shown me that research and creative activity are not only about writing papers, but also about trying ideas, solving problems, and learning from the process.
