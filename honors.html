About the Honors Program

The Honors Program provides students with opportunities to engage in experiential learning and reflect on their growth in areas such as leadership, research, scholarly and creative activity, and intercultural engagement. Through these experiences, students build strong academic and professional skills while developing a deeper understanding of their role in their communities and the world.

The program encourages students to challenge themselves, think critically, and step outside of their comfort zones while documenting their progress through an electronic portfolio.
